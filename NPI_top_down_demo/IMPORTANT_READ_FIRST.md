# IMPORTANT - please read before using these scripts

## For demonstration purposes only

In their current form, these scripts are provided **for demonstration purposes only**. Their aim is to let users repeat
and follow the steps of *Case 1: Tracking impacts on stocks at priority locations* (A-Track D2.2b, top-down
demonstration), to help them understand the **Nature Information Pathways framework**, and to show how ecosystem
accounting principles can be used to generate useful metrics for business decisions from nature data. 

For application in other locations and cases, the scripts can be adjusted to suit the user's goals and local context of the site (for example, using a locally-relevant ecosystem typology and input data, downloading original lookup tables and tailoring to site context, and using ecosystem- and goal-relevant metrics to assess condition).

## Not a black box - tailored to this site and case

- The scripts are **tailored to this site and case**. The concepts and methods apply to a much wider range of settings,
  but the details of the scripts (classes, thresholds, buffers, priority habitats, reference site, etc.) will **not** be
  applicable everywhere.
- Do **not** apply them as a black box to another case without first going through the early steps of the Nature
  Information Pathway, which involve becoming familiar with the **goals and landscape context of your business site**.

## The input data are experimental

- The input maps are the results of an **experimental, predictive mapping approach that is still in development**.
- The inputs **and the outputs** of this repository must **not** be treated as a validated representation of the habitat
  types that are physically present, **nor differences in values treated as ground-measured changes between years**.
- Results (extents, transformations, connectivity, productivity) illustrate the method only and must not be used for
  real decisions, reporting or claims.
